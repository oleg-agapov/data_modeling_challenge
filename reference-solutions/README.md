## How to use them

1. **Solve the task first** and run the task's `checks.sql` until everything passes.
2. **Then compare your reasoning**, not just your SQL. Did you choose the same grain? Did you make the same decisions about cancelled orders, unsold products or NULLs? Where you decided differently, is your choice also defensible?
3. **"Reference" doesn't mean "the only correct answer".** Modeling involves trade-offs. If your model passes the checks and you can explain your decisions, it's a valid solution.

The Task 01 solution is written as a learner's walkthrough, so you can also read it *before* you start, as an example of what a complete solution looks like.

Every solution's SQL was run against the dataset, and passes the task's `checks.sql`.
